# About Theo Marchetti

I'm Theo, a machine learning engineer based in Lyon. At Corvane Bio I fine-tune
graph neural networks for solubility prediction, and I build the checks that tell
medicinal chemists when a model can be trusted. Outside work I boulder twice a week and bake
far too much focaccia.

## What I do at Corvane Bio

Senior Machine Learning Engineer since March 2023; Machine Learning Engineer from September
2021 to February 2023.

- Responsible for fine-tuning graph neural networks to predict solubility.
- Run the weekly model review with the medicinal chemistry team.
- Maintain Tessera, our active-learning platform for assay planning.
- Built the evaluation sets the chemists rely on; every model release is gated on them.

## Before Corvane

From February to August 2021 I was a data science intern at Mirelle Analytics, where I built
demand forecasting for grocery clients.

## Education

I studied applied mathematics at the Halvorsen Institute of Technology and graduated with an
engineering degree (2016–2019). I then did an MSc in Machine Learning at the Université de
Valmont (2019–2020), a double degree with Halvorsen. Before that I spent two years in
preparatory classes in mathematics and physics at Lycée Arbelet (2014–2016).

## Skills
