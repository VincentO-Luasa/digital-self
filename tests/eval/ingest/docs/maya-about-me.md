# About me, the long version

People keep asking how a girl from a fishing town ended up designing hospital software in
Denmark, so here is the long answer. Grab a coffee.

## Norrvik

I was born in 1991 and grew up in Norrvik, a town of four thousand people on the Swedish coast.
My dad repaired boat engines and my mum taught art at the local school, which probably explains
most of what came next. My sister Linnea still lives there and runs the café by the harbour.
I go back every summer.

## Stockholm years

I moved to Stockholm in 2010 to study graphic and interaction design at the Hallström School of
Design. That is where I met Jonas Berg, who sat next to me in typography class and has been one
of my closest friends ever since; he now builds iOS apps and still sends me terrible kerning
screenshots.

After my bachelor's I stayed for a master's in human-computer interaction at the Kungsholm
Institute of Technology (2013–2015). My thesis was about calm notifications for shift workers,
and I think I have been designing for tired people ever since.

My first real job was at Brightwater Studio, a small agency in Södermalm, where I was a junior
UX designer from 2015 to 2017. I made a lot of wireframes for a lot of clients, and learned that
the best research question is usually "show me".

## Rotterdam

In the summer of 2017 I moved to Rotterdam to join Kestrel Mobility, which ran shared scooters
and a journey-planning app in four Dutch cities. I was a product designer in the Rider Experience
team, and my biggest project there was Waypoint, the trip planner we rebuilt in 2019 and 2020 so
that riders could combine a scooter with a tram or a train. We built it on top of the Wayfinder
SDK, which we licensed for its maps and routing; Waypoint itself was all our own work.

Rotterdam is also where Contrast Club started. While I was at Kestrel, my colleague Priya
Natarajan and I kept finding contrast bugs late in the process, so one rainy winter in 2019 we
wrote a small Figma plugin that flags them while you design. It now has around four thousand
weekly users, Priya and I still maintain it together, and it has never made a cent.

I learned Dutch there, badly at first and then well enough to run usability sessions in it.

## Copenhagen

I moved to Copenhagen in the spring of 2021 and have been at Fernhill Health since 2021. Fernhill
makes the apps patients use to book appointments, see test results and message their care team.
I joined as a senior product designer and became lead product designer at the start of 2024.
Today I lead the Design Systems team and Fjord, our design system, which I started in 2022 when
six teams were drawing six different date pickers. My manager, Elena Kovac, is the head of
design and the reason I stayed through a very messy reorganisation.

## Outside work

I have been an urban sketcher for about eight years. I draw on my commute, mostly bridges and
bicycles, and I am not good at it, which is the point. On Thursday evenings I take a pottery
class, and since 2022 I have played the nyckelharpa in a folk band called the Gull Street
Fiddlers. We play weddings, mostly.

I also mentor junior designers through the Design Bridge mentoring network, which I joined in
2020 when I was still in Rotterdam.

## What I believe

I value plain language, honest research and design systems that people enjoy using. I think
accessibility is not a feature but the floor. And I think every designer should spend a day a
month watching people use their product, ideally in the rain.
