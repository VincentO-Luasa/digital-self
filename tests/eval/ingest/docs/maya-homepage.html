Selected work

Projects

Design system · Fernhill Health · 2022–now

Fjord

A shared component library and token set used by six product teams. I started it as a side effort and now lead it full time; it cut the time to ship a new screen by about a third.

Trip planner · Kestrel Mobility · 2019–2020

Waypoint

A redesign of Kestrel's trip planner for riders who combine scooters, trams and trains. Built on top of the Wayfinder SDK, tested with 40 riders in Rotterdam.

Side project · open source · 2019–now

Contrast Club

A free Figma plugin that checks colour contrast against WCAG while you design. Started on evenings while I was at Kestrel; co-maintained with Priya Natarajan.

About

A short version

I grew up in Norrvik, a small town on the Swedish coast, and studied interaction design in Stockholm. After two years at Brightwater Studio, a Stockholm design agency, I moved to Rotterdam to join Kestrel Mobility as a product designer. I've lived in Copenhagen since 2021.

I care about accessibility, plain language and design systems that people actually enjoy using. I speak Swedish, English and Dutch, and enough Danish to order coffee.
