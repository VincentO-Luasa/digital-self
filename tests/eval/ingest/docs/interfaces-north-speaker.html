Maya Lindqvist

Design Lead, Fernhill Health

Talk · 13 November · Main stage · 14:20

Design systems for tired clinicians

Most design systems are built for designers. Fjord, the design system behind Fernhill Health's patient and clinician apps, was built for the nurse finishing a twelve-hour shift. In this talk Maya shows what changed when her team measured components by the mistakes they prevent rather than the screens they ship.

About Maya

Maya Lindqvist is a Swedish product designer based in Copenhagen. She has been with Fernhill Health since early 2021 and now leads its Design Systems team, where she is responsible for Fjord and for the accessibility standards of every patient-facing app.

Before Fernhill she spent almost four years at the Rotterdam mobility start-up Kestrel Mobility, designing the Waypoint trip planner, and began her career at Brightwater Studio in Stockholm. She holds a master's degree in human-computer interaction from the Kungsholm Institute of Technology.

Maya is the co-creator of Contrast Club, an open-source accessibility plugin for Figma, and mentors early-career designers through Design Bridge. When she is not drawing components she is drawing bridges: she is a keen urban sketcher.

This is her first conference talk.
